## Current Strategy
Treat compiler flags as independent variables and isolate one change per official evaluation. Retain the correctness-proven generated wasm2c build and avoid host-shim rewrites until code-generation behavior is understood. Optimize for the aggregate, with special attention to menu because it showed the largest regression under `-O3`.

## Known Failures
An initial run tested `-O3 -DNDEBUG -mcpu=native -fomit-frame-pointer` and scored 698,743 FPS with exact hashes, about 1.4% below its 708,820 preflight; menu regressed most. A follow-up `-O2 -mcpu=native -fomit-frame-pointer` trial scored 712,300 with exact hashes, but the workspace commit was lost to a now-fixed parallel-build cleanup race. Rendering is outside timing, so optimizing raylib or WasmRenderFrame will not help. Fast-math, benchmark edits, skipped frames, fake hashes, and broad gameplay rewrites violate the contract.

## Next Hypothesis
Reproduce the promising isolated configuration by changing only native flags from baseline to `-O2 -DNDEBUG -mcpu=native -fomit-frame-pointer`. The generated wasm2c translation is large and branch/cache heavy, so `-O2` should preserve instruction-cache locality while native CPU targeting and frame-pointer omission provide low-level gains. Do not add LTO, fast-math, WASM flag changes, or host-shim changes in this trial.

## Expected Effect
Reproduce or exceed the prior 712,300 result, with improved aggregate FPS versus the fresh preflight, exact hashes, deterministic passes, and a buildable `native-raylib` target.

## Falsification Signal
The official aggregate fails to improve over fresh preflight/noise, menu remains materially below baseline, build fails, or any correctness/determinism gate changes.
